      Planning notes

      The rules behind your numbers.

      1. 01

        The smallest down payment is 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the part above that, for homes under $1,500,000. At $1,500,000 or more it is 20%. Financial Consumer Agency of Canada

      2. 02

        With less than 20% down, the mortgage is insured. The insurance premium is 2.80% to 4.00% of the mortgage and is added to it. A 30 year amortization adds 0.20%. Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation premiums, Home Start

      3. 03

        In Ontario, 8% provincial sales tax applies to the insurance premium. It cannot be added to the mortgage, so it is paid in cash at closing.

      4. 04

        Lenders test you at the higher of your rate plus 2% or 5.25%. Home costs can be up to 39% of gross income, meaning income before tax, and all debts together up to 44% of gross income. Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions, 8Twelve

      5. 05

        Ontario refunds up to $4,000 of land transfer tax to eligible first time buyers. Government of Ontario

      6. 06

        Toronto rebates up to $4,475 of its municipal land transfer tax to eligible first time buyers. City of Toronto

      7. 07

        The First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 a year and $40,000 in total. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 each, with repayment starting in the fifth year after a first withdrawal made from 2026 to 2028. Canada Revenue Agency, MD Financial Management

      8. 08

        On a brand new home, first time buyers may recover the federal 5% and the Ontario 8% portions of the harmonized sales tax, up to $130,000 on homes up to $1,500,000 bought from a builder between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027. Canada Revenue Agency, Ontario 2026 Fall Statement

      9. 09

        Insured mortgages generally need at least 1 borrower with a credit score of 600 or higher. WOWA

      10. 10

        Rebates assume every buyer is a first time buyer: at least 18, never owned a home anywhere in the world, and moving in within 9 months. Rules checked November 15, 2026.
